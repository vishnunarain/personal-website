This post exists to prove an important property of the site: **the date belongs to the writing, not to Git**.

I can add an article in 2026 and place it accurately in 2021 simply by setting `publishedAt`. Deployment time, commit time, and displayed chronology remain independent.

## The larger principle

Good systems separate concepts that only appear similar.

Publication chronology is one thing. Repository history is another. Last-updated information is a third.
