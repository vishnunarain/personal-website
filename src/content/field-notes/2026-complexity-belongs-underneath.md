Enterprise software often exposes the shape of its backend directly to the person trying to make a decision.

That is usually backwards.

## The interface is a compression layer

A useful product does not merely display all available information. It decides what deserves attention, what can wait, and what relationships matter now.

The harder the underlying system becomes, the calmer the surface should become.
